**Purpose:** Ready-to-send copy for inviting first beta users.  
**Rules:**
- No fake claims
- No unsupported guarantees  
- Honest beta wording
- Lead with their benefit, not our product

## Message 6 — "What to Test" Instructions

**Send this alongside the beta link.**

```
Thanks for joining the Factory beta!

Here's how to get the most out of your 20 minutes:

1. HAVE A REAL IDEA READY
   Don't use our demo ideas — use something you've actually wanted to build.
   Even a simple one: "a booking page for my photography clients."

2. DESCRIBE IT IN YOUR OWN WORDS
   Type it like you'd explain it to a friend. No tech jargon needed.

3. FOLLOW THE FLOW
   Watch the AI agents work. Look at the blueprint. Try to reach the preview.

4. NOTE WHERE YOU GET STUCK
   If something confuses you — write it down. That's the most useful feedback.

5. SHARE IF YOU LIKE IT
   There's a share button on the preview. Try it.

Common questions:
- "Can I save my project?" → Yes, in the workspace section.
- "Is this a real app?" → It's a functional preview. Full deployment is coming.
- "What if I want something different?" → Use the quality/remix tools to iterate.

What NOT to expect (yet):
- Payment processing
- Custom domains
- Full production deployment

If something breaks or confuses you, hit the feedback button (bottom right)
or reply to this message.

Thank you for being part of this — your feedback shapes the product.
```

## Copy Rules

When writing any message about Factory, follow these rules:

**DO say:**
- "Working app preview"
- "Beta — honest and early"
- "AI agents review your build"
- "Shareable preview link"
- "Describe what you want to build"

**DO NOT say:**
- "Production-ready app" (not yet)
- "Guaranteed results"
- "No-code platform" (it's more than that)
- "Replace your developer" (it augments, not replaces)
- "Free forever" (don't make pricing promises)

**Tone:**
- Honest about being in beta
- Specific about what works
- Specific about what doesn't work yet
- Grateful, not pushy
