# Customer Interview Script — Factory Beta

**Interview length:** 20–30 minutes  
**Format:** Video call (preferred) or async written (if async, allow 48h)  
**Who conducts:** Founder  
**When:** Within 48h of user joining beta

---

## Before the Interview

**Prepare:**
- Open the CRM note for this user (`/admin/customer-success`)
- Know their profile: role, what they told you they'd build
- Have a blank note open to capture their words verbatim
- Do NOT demo Factory to them — let them describe what THEY experienced

**Your attitude:**
- You are listening, not defending
- You are curious, not selling
- You are grateful, not apologetic
- Silence is OK — let them think

---

## Opening (2 min)

> "Thanks for taking the time. This really helps us build something useful.
> I'm not going to pitch you anything — I just want to understand your experience.
> There are no wrong answers. The more honest you are, the more you help us."

> "Can I take notes? And is it OK if I quote what you say (without your name) when we share learnings with the team?"

---

## Section 1 — Context (5 min)

**1a. What were you trying to build?**

> "Before you found Factory, what were you actually trying to make happen?
> What problem were you solving or idea were you testing?"

*Listen for: the real use case. Is it specific or vague? Does it match our target profiles?*

---

**1b. What have you tried before?**

> "What did you try before Factory? Fiverr, freelancers, no-code tools, AI tools, building it yourself?"

*Listen for: what they've already paid for. This reveals willingness to pay and existing alternatives.*

---

**1c. What brought you to Factory specifically?**

> "What made you want to try this? What did you think it would do?"

*Listen for: expectations vs reality gap. Misaligned expectations = marketing copy problem.*

---

## Section 2 — Experience (10 min)

**2a. Walk me through what you did.**

> "Tell me what happened from the moment you landed on the site to right now.
> Don't skip the boring parts — I want the honest version."

*Listen for: where they slowed down, what they clicked, when they felt confused or delighted.*

---

**2b. Where did you get confused?**

> "Was there a moment where you weren't sure what to do next?"

*Listen for: specific UI moments, copy that was unclear, features they couldn't find.*

---

**2c. Did the preview concept make sense?**

> "When the system says it's generating a preview of your app — did that mean what you expected?
> What did you think you'd see?"

*Listen for: expectations around fidelity. Many users expect a fully working app — not a preview shell.*

---

**2d. What about sharing or remixing?**

> "If you wanted to show someone else what Factory built — how would you do that?
> Does that concept of sharing a live preview make sense?"

*Listen for: understanding of the share/remix flow. If unclear, it's a copy/UX problem.*

---

**2e. Did anything work better than you expected?**

> "Was there a moment where you thought — oh, that's actually cool?"

*Listen for: the aha moment. This is what we amplify in marketing.*

---

## Section 3 — Value + Willingness to Pay (8 min)

**3a. Would you pay for this? Why or why not?**

> "If Factory worked exactly as well as you'd want it to — how much would you pay per month?
> What plan would make sense for your situation?"

*Listen for: their mental model of value. Is it per project? Per seat? Per output?*
*Important: if they say "I don't know" — ask "what would need to be true before you'd consider paying?"*

---

**3b. What would make this useful for your actual work?**

> "What's the one thing that, if it worked, would make you tell your colleagues about this?"

*Listen for: the missing feature or the core proof point they need.*

---

**3c. What stopped you from going further?**

> "Was there a moment where you felt like — I can't continue right now?
> What caused that?"

*Listen for: blockers. Technical (slow, broken), UX (can't find), emotional (confused, sceptical).*

---

**3d. What would you tell a friend this does?**

> "If a colleague asked you what Factory is in one sentence — what would you say?"

*Listen for: how well our positioning landed. This is pure marketing gold.*

---

## Section 4 — Closing (3 min)

**4a. What should we fix first?**

> "If you were on the team, what's the first thing you'd fix or build?"

---

**4b. Can we reach out again?**

> "Would you be open to a follow-up in 2 weeks to see if anything's changed?
> And could we invite you to a slightly bigger group as we improve?"

---

**4c. Referral**

> "Is there anyone you know who would love something like this — even in its current state?"

## Patterns to Watch For Across Interviews

After 3+ interviews, look for:

| Pattern | Meaning | Action |
|---------|---------|--------|
| 2+ users confused at same step | UX problem | Fix that step |
| 2+ users use same wrong word for a feature | Copy problem | Update that label |
| 2+ users had same "aha" | Core value | Amplify in marketing |
| 2+ users blocked at same point | Blocker | Fix before Wave 2 |
| 2+ users mention same missing feature | Roadmap | Add to Serial 34+ spec |
| 2+ users say "I'd pay for X" | Revenue signal | Prioritise X |
