## Confusion Points

*Where did users get stuck or not know what to do?*

| # | Confusion | Where in the flow | Reported by | Fix needed |
|---|-----------|-------------------|-------------|------------|
| — | — | — | — | Populate after interviews |

**Common confusion zones (anticipate these):**

1. **"Is this a real app?"** — Users expect a fully deployed product, not a preview shell.
   Fix: better copy on the preview reveal step.

2. **"Where do I save my work?"** — Users look for a save button that doesn't exist yet.
   Fix: workspace CTA after demo completes.

3. **"What does 'AI council' mean?"** — Technical term for a non-technical audience.
   Fix: rename to "AI team review" or similar.

4. **"How do I share it?"** — Share button location may not be obvious.
   Fix: make share button more prominent after preview.
