# First 5 Outreach Pack — Factory Beta

**Last updated:** 2026-05-23  
**Status:** Ready to use — personalise `[Name]` and `[LINK]` before sending

---

## Copy Rules (Read Before Sending)

**DO say:**
- "Working app preview from a text description"
- "Early beta — honest and rough in places"
- "Shareable preview link"
- "Takes about 5 minutes"
- "Your feedback shapes what we build next"

**DO NOT say:**
- "Production-ready"
- "Replace your developer"
- "Guaranteed results"
- "Ship in minutes" (we're a preview, not a deploy tool)
- "AI does everything"

**Tone:** Warm, direct, honest. You're a founder talking to someone you respect.  
Not a sales pitch. Not marketing copy. A real ask from a real person.

---

## Message 1 — Short DM (Twitter / LinkedIn / Slack)

*Use as first contact. Under 80 words.*

```
Hey [Name] — I'm running a small beta for Factory, a tool that generates a working app preview from a plain-text description. No code, takes about 5 minutes.

I'm looking for 5 people willing to try it and tell me what didn't work. Would you be up for it? I'll send the link and a few questions — honest feedback only, no pressure to be polite.
```

---

## Message 2 — Follow-Up DM (If No Reply After 5 Days)

*Send once only. Do not follow up again after this.*

```
Hey [Name] — following up on the Factory beta ask from last week.

No worries if it's not a fit right now. If you ever want to try it, the link is [LINK].

Would genuinely value 5 minutes of your time — but no pressure either way.
```

---

## Message 3 — What to Test (Sent With Beta Link)

*Send alongside the invite link. Not a script — a friendly brief.*

```
Here's your beta link: [LINK]

When you open it, try this:
1. Type a real problem you'd want an app for (something you've actually thought about)
2. Click through the demo — watch what happens
3. Look at the preview at the end — does it feel real?
4. Try the share button

Don't try to break it on purpose — just use it like you normally would.

If something confuses you, that's the most useful feedback I can get.
Takes 5–10 minutes. I'll follow up with a few questions after.
```

---

## Message 4 — Interview Request (After They've Used It)

*Send within 24 hours of activation (or if they haven't activated within 48 hours).*

```
Hey [Name] — thanks for trying Factory.

Would you have 20–25 minutes for a quick call? I'd love to hear what you actually experienced — not a sales call, just genuine questions about what worked and what didn't.

I'm asking because your feedback will directly change what I build next.

Available this week? Any time that works for you.
```

---

## Message 5 — Thank You (After Interview)

*Send within 2 hours of interview ending.*

```
[Name] — thanks for the time today. That was genuinely helpful.

The [specific thing they said] is something I'm going to look at this week.

I'll keep you posted as things improve. If you want to try a future version, I'll send it to you first.

No asks — just wanted to say thanks properly.
```

---

## Message 6 — Referral Ask (Optional, After Positive Interview)

*Only send if the interview clearly went well and they expressed genuine enthusiasm.*

```
One more thing — do you know anyone else who builds things without a technical background? Someone who has ideas but hasn't shipped them yet?

I'm looking for 2–3 more people for this wave. A referral from you carries more weight than a cold ask.

No pressure — only if someone comes to mind immediately.
```

## What Not To Do

- Do not send all 6 messages to the same person in sequence — only send what the situation requires
- Do not copy-paste Message 1 to all 5 users without personalising it — they will feel it
- Do not mention competitors or make comparisons — stay focused on Factory
- Do not share pricing in early messages — it's a distraction before they've seen the product
- Do not chase more than twice — if they're not engaging, they're not the right early adopter
